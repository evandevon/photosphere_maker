Photosphere Maker

Make a 360° photo from whatever you have: a phone panorama, photos taken while turning on the spot, game screenshots, or one old photograph. Or fix a 360° photo that's tilted, the wrong shape, or missing its floor and ceiling.

1 Add your pictures

Or press Ctrl+V (Mac: Cmd+V) to paste a screenshot. Paste one at a time, in order.

    Screenshots from Minecraft
    1. Stand in the middle of the room. Press F1 to hide the hotbar and hand. In Options, set FOV to 90.
    2. Face each way exactly with these commands (cheats on), taking a screenshot after each: /tp @s ~ ~ ~ 0 0 (front), /tp @s ~ ~ ~ 90 0 (right), /tp @s ~ ~ ~ 180 0 (back), /tp @s ~ ~ ~ -90 0 (left), /tp @s ~ ~ ~ 0 -90 (up), /tp @s ~ ~ ~ 0 90 (down).
    3. Screenshot: Windows Win+Shift+S · Mac Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4 · Chromebook Ctrl+Shift+Show windows. Paste each one here straight away, in that order.
    4. Choose Six views of a cube and press Lay them out.

    No cheats? Turn by eye, overlapping each picture with the last by about a third, choose Photos taken while turning on the spot, then place them roughly and Snap together.

    2 Place them roughly

    Drag each picture on the flat map to roughly where it belongs; overlap neighbours a little. It doesn't have to be exact: Snap together (step 3) lines them up.

    Choose a picture: click it in the map, or in the list in step 1.

    Trim the edges

    Trim off black borders, a watermark, or a game's on-screen display.

    Keys (with the picture view selected): arrows move it (Shift: further), + − resize, [ ] tilt, Tab next picture.
